Music Player Privacy Policy

App: Music Player (package com.joshtechapps.musicplayer) · Developer: Josh Tech Apps · Effective date: November 24, 2026

In short
  • Your music, playlists, favorites and settings stay on your phone. We do not run a server that collects them.
  • We do not create accounts and we do not sell your data.
  • Some features contact other services: ads (Google AdMob), purchases (Google Play), lyrics (LRCLIB) and online music (Audius). What each one receives is listed below.

1. Who we are

Music Player is an offline-first music and video player for Android made by Josh Tech Apps. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it. If you have a question, write to us at YOUR_EMAIL@example.com.

2. Information stored on your phone

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only. It is not sent to us.

  • Your library: the titles, artists, albums, file locations and durations of songs and videos found on your phone, plus favorites, playlists, play counts, last-played times, bookmarks and the Trash list.
  • Lyrics you saved: lyrics downloaded for a song are saved on your phone so they work offline.
  • Your choices: theme colors, background, light or dark mode, equalizer and sound settings, sleep timer, Settings switches and playback speed.
  • Your background picture: if you pick a photo from your gallery or take one with your camera app for a background, a resized copy is saved inside the app's private storage. It is never uploaded.

Uninstalling the app removes this information. Files you converted with Convert Video to MP3 are saved in your Music folder and stay there until you delete them.

3. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Music and audio, Videos (or Storage on older Android)To find and play the media on your phone.
NotificationsTo show playback controls and, if you leave it on, an alert when a new song is added to your phone.
Microphone (record audio)Optional. Lets the visualizer react to the sound the app itself is playing. The app does not record, save or send any audio.

The app does not hold the camera permission. When you choose to take a background photo, your phone's own camera app opens and hands the picture back. You can turn any permission off in Android Settings, or from the app's Settings screen. Some features then stop working.

When you delete or rename a file from inside the app, Android asks for your permission and the change is made to the real file on your phone.

4. Services that receive information

These services run under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhat it is used forWhat it can receive
Google AdMob Showing banner ads. Ads are removed if you buy Premium. Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google may use these to choose and measure ads.
Google Play Billing The one-time purchase and subscription that remove ads. Handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether you own Premium.
LRCLIB Finding lyrics when you open the lyrics page. The song title, artist, album and length, plus your IP address as part of the internet request.
Audius Online music: search, trending lists and streaming, only when you open Online music. Your search words, the track you play, the app name and key, and your IP address. Album art is also loaded from Audius servers.

You can limit ad personalization in your Google account or in Android Settings under Google → Ads. Android also lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, name or email to use the app.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, messages or photos.
  • We do not upload your music, playlists or listening history.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience music player and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Your library stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Connections to the services above use HTTPS. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Clear everything the app stores: Android Settings → Apps → Music Player → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Turn off new-song alerts and the visualizer in the app's Settings.
  • Move songs to Trash inside the app. Items in Trash are removed automatically after 7 days.
  • Cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check it from time to time.
