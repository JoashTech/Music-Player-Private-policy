Music Player Privacy Policy
Privacy policy for the Music Player Android app (`com.joshtechapps.musicplayer`) by JoshTechApps.
Read it here once GitHub Pages is on: `https://joashtech.github.io/Music-Player-Private-policy/`
Publish with GitHub Pages
Upload `index.html` and this `README.md` to the root of the repository's `main` branch.
Open Settings → Pages.
Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then Save.
Wait a minute. Paste the page address into Google Play Console under App content → Privacy policy.
Before publishing, replace `contactjoshtech@gmail.com` in `index.html` with your real contact email.
